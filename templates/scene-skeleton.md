## The five questions

1. **Whose scene is it?**
2. **What do they want here (concrete)?**
3. **What's in the way?**
4. **What changes by the end (THE TURN)?**
5. **What does the reader know that the characters don't, or vice versa?**

- **If this scene were cut, what would break?**
  (If the answer is "nothing," cut it or give it a job.)

## Post-write checks

- [ ] The turn is actually on screen, not implied
- [ ] No narration echoes dialogue
- [ ] No description of what the sprite already shows
- [ ] No adverbs on speech tags
- [ ] Box lengths vary
- [ ] Important words land at box ends
- [ ] Every character present either speaks or is deliberately, visibly silent
- [ ] The last box is the strongest box
- [ ] Flags set/read here are recorded in the flag map
